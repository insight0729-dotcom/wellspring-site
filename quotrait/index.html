Reading app · Web, Android, iOS

Quotrait

Save the lines you love from books, listen to them again, and share them as cards.

책에서 만난 좋은 문장을 사진으로 저장하고, 다시 듣고, 카드로 나누는 독서 앱입니다.

You no longer need to find the book again to recall a good line. Photograph the page, pick the lines you want, and they are saved as text you can listen to whenever you like.

Get it on Google Play · 구글 플레이에서 받기

Android early access. iOS is in preparation.
안드로이드 얼리 액세스 중, iOS는 준비 중이에요.

Features

주요 기능

Save lines

Photograph a whole page, then tap or drag over the lines you want, or type them in. Line breaks and hyphenation are cleaned up. Scan the barcode to add the book.

페이지를 찍고 원하는 줄만 골라 저장. 바코드로 책 정보 입력.

Listen again

Choose lines to play, set repeats per line or for the whole list, and adjust reading speed and pauses.

저장한 문장을 목소리로 반복해 듣기.

Quote cards

Share a line as a card with a picture drawn for it, or as a paper memo card, in portrait, story and square sizes.

문장에 어울리는 그림을 넣은 카드로 공유.

Your lines, your data

Choose public or private for each line. Export at any time for free as text, Markdown, CSV or a JSON backup. Signing in is optional.

언제든 무료로 내보내기, 로그인은 선택.

What your lines say about you

See your saved lines sorted into six virtues — wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance and transcendence. An estimate, not a diagnosis.

저장한 문장을 여섯 덕목으로 정리.

No ads

Free for up to 100 saved lines. A monthly membership removes the limit. Available in English and Korean.

광고 없음, 무료 100개, 한국어·영어.

Privacy and support

On-device text recognition: photos of book pages are processed on your device and are not uploaded. We do not use ads or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal information.
